Privacy by design

No account. No analytics. No cloud.

MSFI Investor collects nothing about you. There is no sign-up, no email, no device fingerprinting, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting SDK, no third-party analytics, and no cloud sync.

All data — portfolios, transactions, tags, attachments, watchlist, and price alerts — is stored locally in a JSON file inside the app's sandboxed Documents directory. iOS encrypts that directory at rest when the device is locked. The only way data leaves the device is if you tap the Export button and choose to share it.

Frequently asked

Quick answers to the questions we get most often.

Does MSFI Investor connect to my brokerage?

No. MSFI Investor does not connect to any brokerage, exchange, wallet, or trading venue. It never holds funds and never executes trades. Every transaction you record is entered manually.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device in a JSON file inside the app's sandboxed Documents directory. iOS encrypts that directory at rest. Attachments live alongside the snapshot. There is no cloud copy and no backup server.

Are the charts live?

No. Chart series are derived from a deterministic seed keyed to each symbol so the visuals stay stable across launches without requiring a network connection. They are illustrative, not predictive, and are not real-time market data.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Use the Export sheet to download every transaction as CSV, the full app state as JSON for backup or transfer, or a PNG portfolio card. Exports are written to a temporary file you share through the system share sheet.

How does cost basis work?

Weighted average. Buys update the average, sells reduce share count without changing the average, dividends can reduce basis if reinvested, and splits change share count without changing total cost. Realized P/L uses the running average as the sale benchmark.

How do I delete everything?

Open Preferences → Reset All Data, type RESET, and confirm. This permanently deletes every portfolio, transaction, tag, attachment, watchlist item, and price alert on the device.

Is this investment advice?

No. Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy or sell any security, a price target, or a solicitation. You are responsible for your own decisions and for tax accuracy. Consult a licensed advisor for personal guidance.
