Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026 · Last updated: January 1, 2026
TL;DR MSFI Investor collects nothing about you. No account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, no cloud sync, no brokerage link. All data you enter stays on your device until you choose to export it.

1. Who we are

MSFI Investor ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal investment journal published under the name MSFI Investor. For privacy questions, contact us at karlawsokunevajob68@gmail.com.

2. The short version

The App is designed so that we cannot see your data even if we wanted to. There is no account, no sign-in, no server-side component, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracking, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporter. The App makes zero network calls.

  • Your data lives on your device, inside the App's sandboxed Documents directory.
  • iOS encrypts that directory at rest when your device is locked.
  • The only way data leaves your device is if you tap Export and choose to share the resulting file yourself.
  • You can permanently delete everything with Preferences → Reset All Data.

3. Data the App stores on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device, in a JSON file inside the sandboxed Documents directory:

  • Portfolios you create (name, accent color, base currency, target allocation).
  • Transactions you record (date, symbol, type, shares, price, fees, currency, notes, tags, portfolio).
  • Custom tags, labels, and notes.
  • Watchlist entries and price alerts.
  • Display preferences (default currency, decimal digits, haptic feedback on/off).
  • Acknowledgement of the in-app risk disclaimer.
  • Photo attachments you add to journal entries, stored inside the App's sandbox.

This data is never transmitted off your device by the App.

4. Data we do not collect

MSFI Investor does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address, account number).
  • Authentication credentials. There is no login.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IDFA.
  • Usage analytics, session data, or event telemetry.
  • Crash logs, performance metrics, or diagnostic data.
  • Location data, contacts, calendars, or microphone input.
  • Photos except those you explicitly attach to a journal entry.
  • Financial account credentials. The App does not link to any brokerage.

5. Permissions requested by the App

MSFI Investor only requests the iOS permissions it strictly needs:

  • Photos — used solely to attach images you select to a journal entry. The App does not read your photo library automatically.
  • Camera — used solely to capture a photo for a journal entry, only when you tap the camera option.

The App does not request microphone, contacts, calendar, location, Bluetooth, motion, Face ID, push notifications, or background tracking. You can deny Photos or Camera access and still use every feature except photo attachments.

6. Third-party services

The App embeds no third-party SDKs and makes no network calls. Specifically, the App does not use:

  • Google Firebase, Analytics, or Crashlytics.
  • Facebook SDK or advertising pixels.
  • Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, or any product analytics service.
  • Sentry, Bugsnag, or any crash reporter.
  • Any advertising network, ad attribution SDK, or attribution provider.

7. Charts and data sources

Charts and price series in the App are derived from a deterministic seed keyed to each symbol. They are generated locally to keep the visuals stable across launches without requiring a network connection. The App does not connect to any market data provider, exchange feed, or financial API. Charts are illustrative, not live, and not predictive.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children. Because no data is collected at all, this policy applies uniformly to all users.

9. International transfers

Because the App does not transmit data off your device, there are no international data transfers to disclose.

10. Your rights and choices

You control your data entirely:

  • Access — open the App to view every portfolio, transaction, tag, and attachment.
  • Export — use the Export sheet to download transactions as CSV or the full snapshot as JSON.
  • Delete a single item — long-press any portfolio, transaction, tag, or watchlist entry to remove it.
  • Delete everything — Preferences → Reset All Data permanently erases every byte of App data on the device.
  • Uninstall the App — removing the App from your device deletes its sandbox and every file inside.

11. Security

Data is protected by iOS file-system sandboxing and by iOS Data Protection, which encrypts the App's Documents directory at rest when the device is locked. We recommend enabling a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID for the strongest protection. Because we do not operate servers, there is no server-side data to secure and no server breach that could expose your journal.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for changes that materially expand the data we collect, surface a notice inside the App. Because we do not currently collect any data, the most likely reason for a change is a clarification — not a new collection practice.
