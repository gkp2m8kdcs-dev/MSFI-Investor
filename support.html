Frequently asked questions

How do I back up my journal?

The App does not sync to a server. To back up your data, open the Export sheet and download either the Transactions CSV or the full Snapshot JSON. Save the file somewhere safe — iCloud Drive, Files, your computer — so you have a copy outside the App's sandbox.

Can I move my journal to a new iPhone?

Yes. Use the Export sheet to write a Snapshot JSON, then install MSFI Investor on the new device, open the App, and use Import Snapshot from the Preferences screen. Attachments included in the snapshot are restored alongside the data.

How do I delete everything?

Open Preferences → Reset All Data, type RESET, and confirm. This permanently erases every portfolio, transaction, tag, attachment, watchlist item, and price alert on the device. There is no undo.

Where do my photos go when I attach them to a journal entry?

Attached photos are copied into the App's sandboxed Documents directory, inside an attachments/ folder. The originals in your Photos library are not modified or moved. Deleting the App removes the copies.

Does the App work offline?

Yes. MSFI Investor makes no network calls at all. Charts, currency conversion, sector breakdowns, the DCA simulator, and every other feature work fully offline, including on Airplane Mode.

Can I recover deleted data?

No — there is no server copy. If you Reset All Data or uninstall the App, the data is gone. We strongly recommend exporting a Snapshot JSON periodically and storing it somewhere outside the App.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email karlawsokunevajob68@gmail.com with a short description and — for bugs — your iOS version and iPhone model. Screenshots help a lot.

The App crashed. What now?

Please email us with the time of the crash, what you were doing, and your iOS version. Because we do not embed a crash reporter, we rely entirely on user reports to find and fix issues.

Is there a tutorial inside the App?

Yes. The Insights tab contains eight short essays and five graded lessons covering portfolio theory, average cost, drawdowns, factor exposures, and journal hygiene.

Before you write

  • Make sure you are running the latest version of the App from the App Store.
  • Restart your iPhone — many odd behaviors clear after a restart.
  • Reinstalling the App does not delete your data, but resetting the device or clearing the App's Documents folder will.
  • If you are reporting a data loss issue, do not uninstall the App before contacting us.
